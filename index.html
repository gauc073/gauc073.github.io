About Me

I'm an independent developer working across DeFi infrastructure, Rust backend systems and trading technology. I like to understand systems from first principles — why a protocol is shaped the way it is, not just how to call it — and I design and build them end to end: on-chain contracts, the services that sign and relay for them, and the tooling that keeps them running.

Today I'm a core contributor at STBL, a stablecoin project backed by U.S. government securities and RWA assets, where the same protocol design runs on EVM, Stellar and Solana. My focus is minting stablecoin securely against collateral and protecting the treasuries behind it. Before that I spent over three years at Router Protocol, working on the core architecture of the Router chain and then leading development of Nitro, a cross-chain bridge across the EVM, Solana, Cosmos and Sui ecosystems.

I'm open to collaborating on protocol design, infrastructure and security-minded backend work.
